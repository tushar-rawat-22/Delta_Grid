# Commercial direction — strategy falsification, not signals

DeltaGrid's near-term commercial hypothesis is a research-audit workflow, not a signal-selling or trade-recommendation product.

Potential user job:

> Give DeltaGrid a strategy idea or implementation and receive a reproducible, cost-aware evidence package showing assumptions, execution semantics, robustness tests, rejected variants, uncertainty and the exact reasons the idea did or did not survive.

This direction should remain subordinate to current research authority and applicable regulation. It must not claim alpha, make personalized investment recommendations, provide trading calls, or imply paper/live trading authority. Product work should focus on intake normalization, reproducible tests, cost/risk stress, comparison and provenance.
